If I Was 18 Again: My Advice to My Younger Self

#advice #career #investing

If I was literally zapped into a time machine back to when I was 18, before I went to uni, figuring out what I wanted to do — and I wanted to get to where I am today, or get further, literally go from zero pounds saved or invested — here is what I would do differently.

I like to live my life by a no regrets policy. I really think that everything happens for a reason, all that I'm lucky. But I do think that there are some things I could have done to optimise and get to where I am quicker.

Number One

I personally would have chosen an apprenticeship over university. Now I'm saying this as someone that has benefited from my degree. I worked in fashion and it did open a lot of doors for me, but at the time I didn't really know apprenticeships were a thing to me and my values.

Now this makes so much more sense because I am someone that cares about income, being high earning, developing experience like on the ground, having zero student loan and getting ahead financially.

Because actually when I started off in my career, I knew people the same age as me or younger than me on literally double my salary.

Number Two

I would have started posting content so much sooner. I mean, I started at uni but it was just lip syncing videos and like one fashion video — I wasn't really taking it seriously. But had I actually thought about it and utilized the skills I've learnt self-studying personal finance for years and years and years, if I just started making content about it then who knows where I'd be today.

I genuinely think posting content is like investing. You can benefit from compounding. The sooner you start, the more content's out there to be seen and discovered.

Number Three

This is an investing one. And honestly I only did this cause I had limited knowledge, but when I first started investing I was binge watching loads of YouTube videos and reading books about it. I was very into investing into individual stocks, specifically dividend stocks.

Obviously I had a lot more time to do that, but instead of doing that, what I would have done is simply just invested in broad market ETFs and called it a day and not try to actively research stocks. Because I wasn't an expert.

So I was just underperforming the market for a very long time. So when people do see my portfolio now, don't have the highest returns over a long period of time? Because I actually only recently started investing consistently in those specific ETFs.

Number Four

I would have completely bypassed my shopaholic phase or caring about status and logos. I don't even own any of those clothes now, so I feel like it was a complete waste of money.

Instead started saving & investing my money even sooner.

Number Five

This is going to be very controversial because it's gonna seem like I'm not grateful or I don't acknowledge my privilege.

But I probably would have not lived at home after university. Because I would have done an apprenticeship, probably spent a few years at home while doing the apprenticeship, but then moved out.

I personally noticed a huge shift in my confidence, my productivity, what I was able to do — specifically if I had started content creation sooner.

A lot of personal finance advice says to live at home as long as physically possible, and I think, you know, for a lot of people, that's helpful if you're saving from home, if you have the opportunity to do so, that it's great.

But I do think that having that independence and that drive to do more, because you really only have yourself to rely on — I think that's a really powerful thing.

In Summary

In summary, I would have focused on being a high earner sooner and focusing on independence.

Let me know what you think. If you were 18 again, what would you do differently?
