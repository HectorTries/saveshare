If I Was 18 Again: My Advice to My Younger Self

"I would have focused on being a high earner sooner."

By Tori | May 25, 2026
Portfolio £200k in low-cost ETFs
Income £30k+ per month

Mia Rose has built a portfolio of over £66,000 in stocks. She started investing at university, learned through YouTube and books, and now shares her journey with nearly 30,000 followers on TikTok. We met over Zoom to discuss what she'd do differently if she could turn back time.

If I was literally zapped into a time machine back to when I was 18, before I went to uni, figuring out what I wanted to do — and I wanted to get to where I am today, or get further — here is what I would do differently.

She pauses, collecting her thoughts. There is, she says, no regret in her voice — more a sense of pragmatic reflection. She favours a "no regrets policy," she explains, though she acknowledges luck played a part. Still, there are areas she wishes she'd optimised.

The University Question

  1. Choose an apprenticeship over university.
    "Now I'm saying this as someone that has benefited from my degree. I worked in fashion and it did open a lot of doors for me. But at the time, I didn't really know apprenticeships were a thing." She traces this back to her values: income, being high earning, developing experience on the ground, having zero student loan. "When I started off in my career, I knew people the same age as me — or younger — on literally double my salary."

Content as Compounding

  1. Start posting content sooner.
    "I started at uni, but it was just lip-syncing videos. One fashion video — I wasn't really taking it seriously." Had she taken her personal finance knowledge seriously from the start? "Who knows where I'd be today." She leans back. "I genuinely think posting content is like investing. You benefit from compounding. The sooner you start, the more content's out there to be seen."

TheInvesting Trap

  1. Index funds, not individual stocks.
    She laughs, slightly embarrassed. "Honestly, I only did this because I had limited knowledge." Her early investing was YouTube binges and book stacks — heavy into individual dividend stocks. "I had a lot more time then. Instead, what I would have done is simply invested in broad market ETFs and called it a day." She'd not tried to be an expert she wasn't. "I was underperforming the market for a very long time. When people see my portfolio now — I don't have the highest returns over a long period? Because I only recently started consistently in those specific ETFs."

TheStatusTrap

  1. Bypass the shopaholic phase.
    "I don't even own any of those clothes now. It was a complete waste of money." A shake of the head. "Instead started saving and investing my money even sooner."
  2. The independence question.
    "This one's going to be controversial because it's going to seem like I'm not grateful or I don't acknowledge my privilege." She chooses her words carefully. "I probably would not have lived at home after university." An apprenticeship would have meant a few years at home, then moving out. "I noticed a huge shift in my confidence, my productivity, what I was able to do — specifically if I'd started content creation sooner."

"In summary, I would have focused on being a high earner sooner and focusing on independence."

Mia Rose, 26, posts about investing and financial independence on TikTok as @miarosemcgrath.
