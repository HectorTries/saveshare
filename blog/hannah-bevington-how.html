"I'm not trying to sell you something, I'm not trying to give you some quick tips — this is genuinely just how I did it and what worked for me."

Hannah Bevington has become one of the most relatable voices in UK personal finance. Her path to £120K wasn't glamorous or filled with shortcuts. It was grind, sacrifice, and some luck.

The Four Pillars

  1. Increase your income.
    "I'm not saying that I built a six figure business — I'm literally just sold things on Vinted. Every year I would make around £2-3k. This was enough to just keep up my lifestyle."
  2. Save as much as possible.
    "I saved like 80% of my income and this is just what it takes. When you're in your early 20s, you're not earning a lot of money, you have to save like majority of your income."
  3. Live at home.
    "I lived at home. This is genuinely just my story. I know everyone won't be able to relate..."
  4. Time the market (luck).
    "It was literally pure luck in a way, that I started investing during a pandemic when the stocks were crashing. We had some incredibly high years in the stock market after that period, so it meant that my investments just grew massively. And you literally can't predict the stock market."

"I wish I could tell you I just saved like 10% of my income every month and then built a six figure portfolio, but that's just not the truth."

She's brutally honest about the sacrifice required: "I literally just had to sacrifice like nearly all of my income, but I was prepared to do that and that's just kind of what it took. You don't wanna hear it. But that's what I did."
