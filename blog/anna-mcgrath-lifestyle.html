I'm Downgrading My Bougie Lifestyle

#lifestyle #experiment #careerbreak

Hi, I'm Anna, and I am downgrading my bougie life.

Over the years, I've built myself quite a nice life. Premium groceries, expensive coffees, all the subscriptions. But recently I realised something.

The lifestyle's great, but the job I'm doing to pay for it is taking up all of my time and headspace.

So I'm running a little experiment.

I'm downgrading all the little expensive habits and I'm gonna see what's actually worth the money.

The Real Goal

Here's the catch: The goal isn't really to spend less money, it's just to need less money so that I can have a more flexible life in the future.

So I'm taking a career break and I've got one year to:

  • Downgrade my bougie lifestyle
  • Prove to myself that I'm not trapped in a corporate 9 to 5 forever

Let's Find Out

I'm going to document what's actually worth it and what isn't.

The subscription that brings joy vs the one I just forget about. The morning coffee that's worth it vs the one I could live without. The premium groceries that make a difference vs the standard ones that taste the same.

More updates to come...
