5 Ways I Became a Magnet for Opportunity

"Closed mouths don't get fed."

By Tori | May 25, 2026
Portfolio £200k in low-cost ETFs
Income £30k+ per month

Six months ago, Mia Rose was earning £30,000 in a junior fashion role. Today, she's a creative CEO, self-employed, making multiple five-figure months. We asked how she made the leap.

  1. Become inescapable.
    When her content-creator friends see her, they say she's "been everywhere." That's the point, she says. Most people focus on planning, aesthetics, visuals — but forget distribution. She made sure her values and skills appeared on every platform: TikTok, Instagram, Substack, LinkedIn, Spotify. She cites a quote: "Networking is not about who you know — it's who knows you." It's not about being famous, she adds. It's about positioning "where the right people can find you." She leans in. "Let's be honest. Invisibility is expensive."
  2. Shout about your achievements.
    It sounds cringe, she admits — especially for introverts or the shy. "You're not used to digging yourself up." But she's learned something in her "short amount of time being a business owner or content creator": you want to shout about your achievements so much you become "borderline annoying."
  3. Practice transparency.
    Not necessarily numbers — "that might be a step too far for most people and probably not advised." But transparency means vulnerability. Putting your cards on the table. Most people gatekeep because they fear others will take their ideas and "get ahead." But "in real life that is not the case." Her experience: "The more you help people, the more you get ahead."
  4. Show up every single day.
    "When I had pimples, when I looked my worst, when I was exhausted, when I didn't want to film — I did it anyway." Even now as a full-time creator, she balanced a nine-to-five with daily posting. That's not easy, she acknowledges. But she made sure those videos were "highly engaging." She got ahead because "when you're willing to do something that not many people are, you just end up outpacing everyone." Content, she says, is "a game of who can actually last the longest — not necessarily who has the most followers or engagement."
  5. Shout about your desires.
    The quickest way to manifest anything, she believes, is telling people what you want. She announced financial milestones and goals on TikTok. Every time she's set a goal, it's come true — "not out of luck or just randomness, because I set the intention and then every single day I was working towards it." The key: when you tell people what you want, "they're able to help you." There's that famous phrase: "Closed mouths don't get fed." Most people don't get what they want "not because they're not talented, but because they don't ask for it."

"The more you help people, the more you get ahead."
