Your debts, your mountains

Each debt is one mountain: a peak, a right slope down to the payoff flag, and a left slope into the spiral pit. Your snowball starts at the peak. APR is gravity — it constantly pulls you toward the spiral. Every payment pushes you down the payoff slope, and the earlier you pay, the more the terrain flattens ahead of you, permanently.

How it works: tap a mountain, pick your payment, then time the marker into the gold on the power bar. Perfect timing = a bigger push. Principal moves the ball 1:1 with real £ — interest avoided is shown separately and permanently flattens the slope ahead. No luck, no loot boxes: only your payments and your timing.
