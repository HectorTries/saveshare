SNOWBALL • 100 LEVELS

Your debts, 100 levels each

Every debt is split into 100 levels — each level is 1% of the balance (a £10,000 debt is £100 a level). Your snowball rolls down the slope and clears a level with every 1% you pay. Add each debt's monthly repayment for honest amortisation maths. No timing, no luck — just your money working through the levels.

Tip: list your debts from smallest to largest if you're following the classic snowball method — the game's strategy toggle will guide you either way.
