Your debts, your hills

Each debt is one hill. Your snowball starts at the top and every payment rolls it further down toward the payoff flag. Each payment also grows the ball — and a bigger ball rolls faster. Add each debt's monthly repayment and the game uses real amortisation maths: the months left and the interest you avoid are honest. No timing, no luck, no streaks — the ball waits patiently for your next payment.

How it works: enter each debt with its balance, APR, term (in months or years) and monthly repayment. Pick a payment amount, tap a hill, press PAY. Every £ of principal moves the ball down the hill — 1:1 with the % of the original balance paid, so a mortgage and a credit card are measured the same honest way. High APR adds a little hill friction, and long debts (like a mortgage) roll far slower than short ones. The interest you avoid is shown separately as its own number — it never inflates your progress.
